Morié

The flagship blend

Morié, our signature moringa infusion.

Morié is brewed from carefully sourced moringa leaves, dried and blended for a smooth, grassy warmth. No shortcuts, no artificial notes — just a cup that tastes like where it came from.

  • Grown and dried close to source
  • Blended in small batches
  • Brews in minutes, no bitterness

Our Story.

BrySam Botanicals began with Bryan and Samuel, two friends who wanted a botanical brand that felt honest — rooted in Nigerian soil, but built with a modern hand. Morié is where that idea first took shape: a simple infusion, made with real ingredients and real care.

What started as a shared conversation about better, simpler brewing has grown into a brand built to last — one cup, one harvest, one season at a time.
